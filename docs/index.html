    Sage-Interaction

    An open-source duplex model built for real-time voice and video interaction with background task delegation.

    Summary. Sage-Interaction reaches 80.73 Interaction Quality at 1.29 s Turn Taking Latency on the supplied comparison. Voice results show strong interruption and resumption behavior. Sage-Interaction has the highest F1 on Daily Delegation and the second-highest on Time Speak. It leads the displayed video comparisons on Charades, ProactiveQA, OmniVideoBench, and LVOmniBench.

    Frontier Full-Duplex Model

    A strong quality–latency trade-off: The frontier plot compares the FD-Bench v1.5 average with FD-Bench v1 turn-taking latency. Sage-Interaction scores 80.73 at 1.29 s, combining the highest interaction-quality score with the second-lowest latency in this plotted comparison. Moss-Live scores 79.28 at 1.47 s, while Realtime-Venus reaches 78.84 at 2.37 s. The other plotted models range from 30.14 to 79.28 in interaction quality and from 1.19 to 2.37 s in latency. Together, these results show a strong balance between responsive turn-taking and sustained interaction quality.

    Audio Benchmarks

    State-of-the-art interaction quality with fast, resilient turn-taking: On FD-Bench v1.5, Sage-Interaction achieves an average score of 80.73, the highest among the models compared. Its component scores are 83.00 for responding to user interruptions, 96.94 for resuming after a backchannel, 82.00 for resuming after background speech, and 61.00 when users are talking to others. On FD-Bench v1, it scores 84.87 for turn-taking outcome with 1.29 s latency, faster than Doubao-Duplex (1.39 s), Moss-Live (1.47 s), and Realtime-Venus (2.37 s); Qwen3.8 is faster at 1.19 s. Overall, Sage-Interaction combines the highest average interaction quality in this comparison with competitive turn-taking speed and resilient conversational continuity.

    General Knowledge Quality: Sage-Interaction scores 83.73 on Llama Questions, above Doubao-Duplex (82.47) and Qwen3.5-Plus (81.20), and close to the best score among the displayed open-source baselines (84.67). On AlpacaEval, it achieves 4.40/5, ahead of the other listed open-source models, which score between 3.53 and 4.26. These results show that Sage-Interaction maintains strong knowledge and response quality alongside its high interactivity.

    Video benchmarks

    Sage-Interaction records 21.6 mIoU on Charades, 42.3 PAUC on ProactiveQA, 69.1 accuracy on StreamingBench, 52.9 accuracy on WorldSense, 41.1 accuracy on OmniVideoBench, and 40.5 accuracy on LVOmniBench. It leads the displayed comparisons on Charades, ProactiveQA, OmniVideoBench, and LVOmniBench. Together, these results highlight Sage-Interaction's strong video understanding and proactive response capabilities.

    Delegation benchmarks

    On Daily Delegation, Sage-Interaction achieves 80.47 F1, the highest score in the supplied comparison, with 4.13/5 quality. On Time Speak it records 85.96 F1 at 1.019 s latency. GPT-2.1 has the highest F1 (90.61) and is slightly faster (0.921 s); Sage-Interaction's F1 is close to GPT-2.1-mini (83.33) while its latency is substantially lower than that model's 2.649 s. These results show effective delegation in daily interactions and responsive performance on timed speech tasks.
